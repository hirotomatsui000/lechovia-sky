## 2026-10-06: G blackout (spec revision 21)

- The owner: "G builds → the view goes red and darker → at its darkest the pilot blacks out → the stick stops
  answering and the jet crashes". `shared/physics/g-tolerance.ts`: a strain 0 … 1 that builds above 7 G
  (`((n − 7)/2)^1.5/10` per s: 10 s at 9 G) and drains below; the World keeps it on each aircraft (`gStrain`,
  `blackoutTick`), emits `blackout` at 1, then replaces the pilot's input with a slumped stick (pull 0.3, rolled to
  150° toward the low wing, no buttons) until the crash, a new death cause `blackout` (credited like a crash).
  `World.blackedOutS`, `AircraftView.gStrain/blackedOutS`.
- Client: `hud/g-vision.ts` (red, darkness closing in from the edges, black at 1; after G-LOC 2 s black with `G-LOC
  · BLACKED OUT · NO CONTROL`, then the fall 60% dark), drawn over the scene and HUD but under the messages; the G
  readout turns amber/red; the HUD symbology is gone while blacked out; death text `BLACKED OUT (G-LOC)`, kill feed
  "blacked out and crashed" / "forced a G-LOC"; falling-wreck effect skipped (it hits the ground). Replaces M1's
  cosmetic vignette (> 7 G for 2 s).
- AI: `BotPilot.think` caps the pull at half stick past strain 0.55 (0.9 while breaking from a missile or pulling
  off the ground). Tuned with the tournament: a 6 G tolerance with easing at 0.6 put 4 pairings outside 35–65%,
  smoother easing 12–13; 7 G and 0.55 put all 16 inside over seeds 1–100 (1,021 draws). Seeds 1001–1300: 5 outside
  against 4 before this change (the same weak pairings: Kestrel/Condor vs Prizrak/Yastreb, Tempest–Yastreb).
- Measured: a full-stick turn from 300 m/s at 3 km takes the view at about 4 s and blacks out at 11–13 s; the slumped
  jet hits the ground about 15 s later from 3 km, 28 s at most from 8 km. Bots: no blackouts in 60 3-minute matches;
  Aces fly with some view gone 40% of the time, Veterans 6%, Rookies almost never.
- Tests: `g-tolerance.test.ts`, `world/blackout.test.ts` (a held turn blacks out, inputs ignored, `blackout` crash,
  clear head after respawn; easing off drains it; Ace bots never black out; without the easing 8 do),
  `g-vision.test.ts`, death text and kill feed.
